# 05 — Strengths & weaknesses audit (current web presence)

Scope: aussiefitnessbooks.com (Shopify), aussiefitness.info (Beacons) and the AussieFit app at aussiefit.vercel.app.

| Area | Score /10 | Evidence | Opportunity | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand clarity & differentiation | 6 | Story is strong in press (NZ Herald) but absent from the store, whose homepage has no tagline or bio | Lead with "−60kg on fakeaways" | High |
| Visual identity | 3 | No logo file, colours or type system found; default Shopify theme | Define a system (06) | High |
| UX & navigation | 4 | Visitors hop Beacons → Payhip or Shopify (two stores, same books) | One home with one path | High |
| Conversion path | 5 | Permanent "50% OFF LIMITED TIME" banners; the meal prep book shows "Sold out" on Shopify | Honest pricing; app subscription plus books in one place | High |
| Content quality | 7 | 300+ real recipes with macros; viral formats | Recipe pages as SEO landing pages | High |
| Trust & social proof | 5 | Real press exists but isn't shown anywhere; no reviews on the store | "As featured in" with links to the articles | Med |
| Mobile experience | 6 | Shopify and Beacons are responsive but generic | Mobile-first editorial design | Med |
| Performance | ? | Not measured on third-party sites (no Lighthouse access to the live Shopify) | New site target is 90+ | Med |
| SEO | 2 | No indexable recipe pages anywhere; app used `/?recipe=` URLs | 135 SSR recipe pages with Recipe schema | High |
| Accessibility | ? | Not audited on third-party sites | WCAG 2.2 AA on the new site | Med |
| Social media presence | 9 | Millions of followers across TikTok/IG (sources disagree on the exact count) and national press | Funnel that attention into owned channels | — |

## Top 5 strengths to amplify
1. A real, press-verified 60kg transformation.
2. The fakeaway-swap concept: instantly understood and shareable.
3. A large, real recipe library with macros.
4. A huge social reach.
5. Products already selling (4 ebooks plus the app).

## Top 5 weaknesses to fix
1. No owned home that tells the story.
2. Two parallel stores (Shopify and Payhip) with the same products.
3. No visual identity.
4. Zero search visibility for recipes.
5. "Limited time" sale pressure that is never limited (erodes trust and may breach the ACL on misleading pricing; **legal review recommended**).

## Single biggest opportunity
Turn social attention into **owned, compounding traffic**. 135 indexable recipe pages each funnel into one offer (the app), with the ebooks as an upsell, all under one brand.
